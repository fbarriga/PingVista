# PingVista Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 18, 2026

PingVista is developed by Felipe Barriga Richards ("we", "us", or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how PingVista handles information when you use the app.

## Information PingVista handles

PingVista does not require an account and does not include advertising or a developer-operated backend. We do not sell or rent your personal information. We disclose the analytics information described below to Google, which processes it to provide Firebase Analytics.

PingVista uses Firebase Analytics (provided by Google) to understand app usage and improve the app. It records automatically collected events, such as app opens and sessions, and events when a ping test starts or finishes. Test events may include the protocol, port, address family, packet size, TTL, interval, requested and completed ping counts, cancellation and packet-loss counts, latency summary statistics, and whether you stopped the test. PingVista does not send the target host, IP address, or URL to Firebase Analytics.

Firebase Analytics may also collect an app-installation identifier, approximate location derived from the network address, and general device, operating-system, app-version, and session information. PingVista does not set an account identifier or other user ID in Firebase Analytics. Google processes this information under [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [Firebase's privacy and security terms](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy). Analytics is separate from the diagnostic network traffic described below.

To perform the network diagnostics that you request, PingVista sends network traffic from your device to the target you select. This may include a host name, IP address, URL, port, address family, and the contents needed for the selected ICMP, HTTPS, TCP, or UDP/STUN test. The receiving service necessarily receives your device's network address and other information ordinarily included in a network connection, such as connection time and request metadata. That service handles information under its own privacy policy.

PingVista also contacts `ifconfig.me` and, if that request fails, `api.ipify.org` to display your external IP address on the Info screen. The app does not send that result to us or store it after the app is closed.

Built-in diagnostic targets include services operated by Cloudflare, Google, Quad9, Apple, Mozilla, and the operators of the `ifconfig.me` and `api.ipify.org` services. You may instead choose a target you control. We do not control third-party services, their availability, or their privacy practices.

## Storage and retention

PingVista keeps detailed, individual ping results and network information only in the app's memory while it is running. It does not create an account or retain those detailed results on a developer-operated server.

The usage events and aggregated test statistics described above are sent to Firebase Analytics and retained by Google according to the retention settings for PingVista's Analytics property and Google's applicable terms. Google provides controls for retention and deletion of Analytics data.

## Your choices

You can avoid sending a diagnostic request by not starting a test. You can choose a target you control instead of a built-in target. You can avoid an external-IP lookup by not opening or refreshing the Info screen.

PingVista does not currently provide an in-app switch for Firebase Analytics. Uninstalling the app stops future collection from that installation. You may contact us with privacy questions or requests using the address below.

## Security

HTTPS requests use HTTPS. Other diagnostic protocols have the network security characteristics of those protocols and are intended for network measurement, not for transmitting sensitive information.

## Children's privacy

PingVista is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when PingVista's data practices change. The effective date above will be updated when we do.
